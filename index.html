          About Geo

          What it is

          Geo is a free flashcard app for learning where things are. Each card shows a small map with one place highlighted: a country, an ocean or sea, a lake, an island, a mountain range, a desert or a region. Name it, then flip the card to check. For countries, the back also shows the capital, marked on the map.

          What you can do

          • Learn a few new places each day: 10 by default, adjustable in Settings.
          • Choose what to study: countries and capitals, oceans, seas and lakes, islands, or mountains, deserts and regions. Mix them or focus on one.
          • Choose the order new places come in: Mixed (the default), Best known first, or Random.
          • Review each place just before you’re likely to forget it. This is called spaced repetition: places you know well come back less and less often.
          • Rate each card Again, Hard, Good or Easy. Your answer decides when you’ll see it next.
          • Browse or search all the places in the List, and tap any of them to see it on the map.
          • Follow your streak, mastery and upcoming reviews in Progress.
          • Use it offline, or install it on your phone or computer. There’s no account: your progress stays in this browser, and you can export a backup from Settings.

          About the maps

          The maps are drawn from Natural Earth, a public-domain map dataset. Borders follow Natural Earth’s convention of showing who controls an area on the ground, which is not an endorsement of any claim. Shapes for seas, mountain ranges, deserts and regions are approximate: their edges are rarely agreed precisely. Capitals were checked in November 2026.

          Credits

          Map data: Natural Earth (public domain). Map projection: d3-geo and topojson-client (ISC license). Card selection, names, notes and capitals were written for this app.
